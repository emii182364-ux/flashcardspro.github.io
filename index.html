Data Collection

The app does not collect any personal data. Your flashcards, decks, study statistics, images and audio recordings are stored on your device and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud account. Nothing is sent to servers operated by the developer.

Sign in with Apple

You can optionally sign in with your Apple Account to enable iCloud sync. Apple provides the app with a user identifier and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share (which may be Apple's private relay address).

This information is stored only on your device and is used to display your account inside the app. It is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Signing out removes it from the device.

iCloud Sync

If enabled, your flashcards, decks, statistics, images and audio are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer has no access to this data.

You can disable sync in the app, and delete the data at any time from Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Permissions

The app requests these permissions only when you use the related feature. You can change them at any time in Settings.

  • MicrophoneTo record audio for your flashcards and to listen to your pronunciation in voice study mode. Recordings are stored on your device and in your iCloud account.
  • Speech RecognitionTo check your pronunciation of flashcard answers. Provided by Apple; audio may be processed by Apple's servers according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives the audio or transcripts.
  • CalendarTo display today's events in the Calendar widget. Events are read on the device and are not stored or transmitted anywhere.
  • PhotosTo add images to your flashcards. The app only receives the pictures you explicitly select.

Weather Widget

The clock widget can show a short weather forecast. It derives an approximate city from your device's time zone — the app never uses GPS or Location Services — and requests the forecast from Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Only the city name or approximate coordinates are sent, with no account or identifier.

News

The News tab downloads a public, read-only file hosted on GitHub and may load video thumbnails from YouTube. No information about you is sent with these requests other than what is technically necessary to download the content (such as your IP address).

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app receives your flashcards directly from your iPhone and keeps a local copy on the watch. It does not connect to the internet.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or handle payment information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Analytics, Tracking & Sharing

The app uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites. No user data is sold or shared with third parties. The only outside services the app contacts are:

AppleiCloud, speech recognition, purchases
Open-MeteoWeather forecast

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
