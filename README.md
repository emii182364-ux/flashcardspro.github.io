<h1>Privacy Policy - Flashcards Widget Pro</h1>
<p>Last updated: September 13, 2026</p>

<h2>Data Collection</h2>
<p>Flashcards Widget Pro is designed with privacy in mind. All content you create — flashcards, audio recordings, and images — is stored exclusively on your device or your personal iCloud account. The developer has no access to this data.</p>

<h2>Sign in with Apple</h2>
<p>The app offers optional Sign in with Apple for iCloud sync. If you choose to sign in, your name and email address are stored locally on your device only. These are never transmitted to any developer server. You may use Apple's private email relay — your real address is never required.</p>

<h2>Microphone</h2>
<p>If you choose to record audio for your flashcards, the app requests access to your microphone. Recordings are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account. No audio data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.</p>

<h2>Photos</h2>
<p>If you choose to add images to your flashcards, the app requests access to your photo library. Images are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account. No photo data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.</p>

<h2>iCloud Sync</h2>
<p>If enabled, your flashcard data is stored in your own iCloud account using CloudKit. The developer has no access to it.</p>

<h2>In-App Purchases</h2>
<p>Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. The developer does not handle or store any payment information.</p>

<h2>Analytics and Tracking</h2>
<p>The app uses no analytics SDKs or tracking tools. NSPrivacyTracking is false. No data is shared with advertising networks.</p>

<h2>Data Sharing</h2>
<p>No user data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties under any circumstances.</p>
